How gifts work
  • Illegal gifts are taken only by someone who is shady, and only their one favorite. Addicts also take their favorite drug and just laugh off a different drug.
  • Wrong illegal gift: 1st refusal is harmless, 2nd costs mood and attitude, 3rd costs a lot of attitude and offends them.
  • Security agents refuse every illegal gift from the gift screen. A shady agent still values their favorite at 3× in contract negotiations.
  • Legal gifts are taken by everyone. The same legal gift gives its full boost twice a year per person; a third within 365 days costs 5% attitude instead.
  • Favorites never change on their own. Becoming an addict switches someone to shady with a drug favorite.
  • Offended people can't receive gifts.
